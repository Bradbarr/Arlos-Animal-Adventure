# Arlo's Animal Adventure — Changelog

## Version 4

### Animals and Story
- **20 new animals, so there are 30 in total.** Each new animal has baby and grown-up pixel art, a voice, a fact, a reading question, a spelling word, a size and Sanctuary needs.
  - The ladybird and bumblebee babies are larvae, which teaches the life cycle.
  - The frog baby is a froglet.
  - The grey seal pup is white, as real ones are.
- **20 new landscapes**, each with its own ground, sky, hiding places and decoy creatures.
- **Three chapters of 10 landscapes each:**
  - Chapter 1, Wild Rescue: the original 10.
  - Chapter 2, Wild Isles: British woods, rivers and gardens.
  - Chapter 3, Shores and Far Lands.
- **Chapter rules:**
  - Each chapter unlocks when the one before it is finished.
  - Each chapter's trail is kept, so you can switch between chapters without losing progress.
  - A saved game that had already finished the old story opens Chapter 2 straight away.
- **Wild Week:** after Chapter 3, a trail of 10 surprise landscapes drawn from every chapter. A new trail arrives each week.
- **Ranger Signs:** a blue sign with a question mark in each zone. Each one asks an optional quick question worth 3 treasures.
- **Coins:** rescuing a baby earns 2p and finishing a chapter earns 10p.
- **Stickers** for:
  - the Hideout (Hideout Hero, and Master Code Cracker after 5 rescues)
  - each chapter
  - each Wild Week
  - Release Day
  - your first look under the sea

### Learning
- **A new strand, Nature and Science**, with four tasks:
  - where animals live
  - what they eat (plant-eater, meat-eater or both)
  - how they grow (life cycles)
  - how to be a nature helper
- **New maths tasks:** Make Ten (number bonds to 5, 10 and 20), Count the Coins (including paying an exact amount), and What Time Is It? (o'clock, half past and quarter past or to).
- **New reading tasks:** First Sounds (phonics) and Tricky Words.
- **Learning plan:** each 10-landscape trail now has maths, reading and nature twice each, and the other strands at least once. The same strand never comes twice in a row.
- **Skill tracking:** stars are recorded for every task. A skill with low stars comes back sooner, both at Task Gates and in Today's Practice.
- **Today's Practice** (on the main menu): three tasks, with shaky skills first.
- **Grown-ups page:**
  - It sits behind a 3-second hold and a times-table question.
  - It shows skills per task, flags shaky skills, and gives totals for animals, homes, chapters and coins.
  - It has switches for "unlock every chapter" and night raids.

### Sanctuary
- **Bigger map:** it grows from 64 × 64 to 96 × 96 tiles. An older sanctuary keeps everything and gains the new areas around its edge.
- **New areas, all with marked borders:**
  - building areas: Woodland Park, Great Plains, Beach and Open Sea
  - gathering areas: Wild Wood, Heather Moor and Red Outback
- **Getting around:**
  - a minimap in the corner
  - a full map where you tap to travel
  - zoom with ＋ and −, or the − and = keys
- **New materials and objects:**
  - leaf litter, heather, pebble and red sand blocks
  - birch, hazel (nuts), heather (nectar), mushrooms, shells, seaweed and long grass
- **New things to make**, which can need boulders and reeds as well as wood: boulder caves, sett tunnels, nest boxes, bug and bee hotels, and rock pools.
- **Nocturnal animals nap by day.** The fox, badger, dormouse, barn owl and hedgehog sleep in a den in the middle of the day. Tap the den to peek in and see them asleep.
- **Biomes:** each pen shows the biome its floor makes (woodland, savannah, heath, wetland, sea and so on) and whether the animal feels at home.
- **Happy Points meter:** out of 10. Points come from decorations (more for an animal's favourites), the right biome and a full bowl.
- **Room by size:** new animals need room to match their size: small 12 tiles, medium 16, large 30 and giant 50.
- **Fence styles:** wood rails, picket, stone wall or hedge, in any paint colour.
- **Free gravel paths:** the new PATH mode in the fence tool lays them.
- **Visitors** walk your paths and leave coins at happy homes.
- **Food Store:** buy food with coins by tapping out the exact amount.
- **Ranger Jobs board:** three sums about your Sanctuary each day, worth 6p.
- **The sea:**
  - a second boat waits at the beach
  - fences drawn on the sea become rope nets with buoys
  - signs and feeding baskets float
  - the nurse shark needs a net pen of at least 80 sea tiles
- **Look Below:** an underwater view with labelled sand, rocks, seaweed and coral, fish, bubbles, and your sea animals. Tap an animal for its fact.
- **Release Day:** grown sharks, seals and sea turtles can go back to the wild. A new baby then arrives at the nursery.

### Fixes
- The backup download button is gone, because the published viewer blocks downloads. The copy-paste save code still works.
- Test shortcuts (the ` key) only work when tests switch them on.
- Correct plurals: foxes, dormice and starfish.

### Version 4.1: treasures you can spend
- **The 💎 treasures from the Story are now money for the Sanctuary.** They still give a hint for every 5 collected, and they are also saved in a wallet. Treasures already collected in earlier versions count towards it.
- **The Ranger Hut is now a Treasure Shop.** Tapping it no longer gives free meat. It opens a shop that takes 💎 only:

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Meat ×3 | 3 💎 |
| Saplings ×3 | 6 💎 |
| Wood ×10 | 8 💎 |
| Fences ×10 | 8 💎 |
| Boulders ×4 | 10 💎 |
| Treasure chest | 20 💎 |
| Swing | 30 💎 |
| Fountain | 40 💎 |
| Rainbow arch | 50 💎 |
| Golden Arlo statue | 75 💎 |

  The chest, swing, fountain, arch and statue are new decorations. They count towards Happy Points.
- **Paying is a subtraction question.** Each purchase asks how many 💎 will be left, for example 45 − 40.
- **Treasures sparkle back.** In a landscape you have finished, collected treasures return after 10 minutes, so it is worth going back to explore. Ranger Signs still give 3 💎 each.
- **Explained in the game:**
  - a first-treasure message in the Story
  - a "Ranger Hut is now a Treasure Shop" message in the Sanctuary
  - entries in How to Play for both modes
  - a "Go treasure hunting" button in the shop
  - the 💎 total on the menu, the bag and the Sanctuary bar
