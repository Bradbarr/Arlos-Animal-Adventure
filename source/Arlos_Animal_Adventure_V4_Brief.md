# Arlo's Animal Adventure: V4 brief (reviewed and extended)

Attach index.html (V3) and the src/ folder with build.sh. Read all of it before changing anything. Keep everything that works. Arlo (age 6) and his dad built this together. He loves the landscape levels, finding the hidden animal, and the Sanctuary where animals are nurtured and grow. Build on those. Don't replace them.

## What changed from the first draft, and why

- **Hedgehog is already in the game.** It is one of the original 10, so the new 20 drop it. The fox takes its place.
- **There are 30 animals, not 40.** That is the original 10 plus 20 new. Every "all 40" in the draft now reads "all 30".
- **Stoat swapped for kangaroo.** The British chapter is already full of small mammals, and the far-lands chapter needed a land animal from a new continent (Australia).
- **Starfish and nurse shark live only in water.** The draft treated them like land animals. They now need water tiles to move on.
- **The learning plan was impossible as written.** It had seven strands but kept "every strand twice in 10 levels", which needs 14 levels. Nature becomes a sixth strand. Maths, reading and nature each appear twice per chapter, and the other three appear at least once.
- **"Read a sum aloud" can't be checked by the game.** The grown-up gate is now a hold-to-open button plus a times-table question with four answers to tap.
- **The levels become chapters.** Twenty new levels added to the old trail would make one 30-landscape walk, which is too long for a 6-year-old. There are three chapters of 10 instead. Each chapter unlocks after the one before, and a grown-up can unlock them all.
- **Wild Week runs weekly, not daily.** A new 10-landscape trail every day is more than a child can finish, so the trail changes each week.
- **The Food Store needs something to pay with.** Coins (1p, 2p, 5p and 10p) are added. Arlo earns them from visitors and Ranger Jobs, and pays by tapping coins to make the exact amount. That makes the shop a money lesson.

## 2. Twenty new landscapes

Each new landscape keeps the existing structure: three zones, a moving baby, clues, treasure, the Poacher, the Hideout and the Task Gate. Each has its own ground, sky, hiding places, decoys and weather effects.

## 3. Learning that never stops

There are six strands: Maths, Reading and Spelling, Problem Solving, Feelings, Drawing, and a new Nature and Science strand. The new tasks are:

- **Maths:** number bonds to 10 and 20, paying with coins in pence, and o'clock and half past on a clock face.
- **Reading:** phonics (the first sound of a word) and tricky words.
- **Nature:** where an animal lives, what it eats (plant-eater, meat-eater or both), life cycles, and how rangers protect endangered animals.

Learning happens in four places:

1. **The Task Gate** at the end of every landscape.
2. **Ranger Signs:** one optional quick question in each zone, worth +3 treasure.
3. **Ranger Jobs** in the Sanctuary: sums about Arlo's own animals and materials. These earn coins.
4. **The Food Store:** pay the exact price in coins.

The game also adapts to Arlo:

- **Skill tracking:** stars are recorded per task. Shaky skills come back sooner in Today's Practice and at the gates, which is gentle spaced repetition.
- **No pressure:** there are no streaks, nothing is ever lost, and a wrong answer is never shamed.

## 4. An endless world

After Chapter 3, **Wild Week** opens. Each week a new trail of 10 landscapes is chosen from the 30, so there is always a fresh one.

A **Grown-ups page** shows:

- skills and stars per task, with shaky skills flagged
- animals rescued and homes built
- a switch to unlock all chapters

Nothing leaves the device.

## 5. A bigger Sanctuary

**Map and getting around**

- The map is 96 × 96 tiles, more than twice the old 64 × 64. An old sanctuary sits inside the new map unchanged, so nothing is lost.
- There are new named areas, all with visible borders:
  - Wild Wood (leaf litter, birch, mushrooms)
  - Woodland Park, a build area
  - Heather Moor
  - the Great Plains, a big build area for giant animals
  - Red Outback
  - the Beach and Rock Pools
  - the Open Sea
- A minimap sits in the corner.
- A Map screen shows every area. Tap an area to travel there.
- Zoom in and out with the ＋ and − buttons.

**Building**

- **Materials:** recipes can use wood, boulders, reeds, shells and other things you gather.
- **Boulder caves:** need 4 boulders. Nocturnal animals (badger, fox, dormouse, hedgehog and barn owl) sleep inside by day. Tap a cave to peek in.
- **Biomes:** the ground inside a pen decides its biome (woodland, wetland, heath, meadow, savannah, beach or rocky). Each animal shows whether it feels at home.
- **Decorations:** at least six per habitat type, such as mushrooms, a bird box, a bug hotel, seaweed, shells, an acacia tree and a sett.
- **Happy Points:** a meter that rises with decorations, more so for ones that suit the animal.
- **Fence styles:** wood, picket, stone wall or hedge, in any paint colour.
- **Path tool:** drag to lay gravel paths.

**Visitors, coins and jobs**

- Visitors walk the paths and leave coins at happy homes.
- The Food Store sells every food for coins.
- The Ranger Jobs board gives sums about Arlo's own animals and materials, and pays coins.

**The sea pen and the nurse shark**

- **No land tank.** A second boat waits at the beach. Fences drawn on sea water become rope nets with buoys.
- **Size:** the shark needs a net pen of at least 80 sea tiles with a gate. Its sign and feeding basket float on the water.
- **Feeding:** fill the basket with fish from the boat.
- **Look Below:** an underwater view with sand, rocks and seaweed, all labelled. The nurse shark rests on the seabed with fish, crabs and starfish around it. Drag to look around and tap an animal for a fact. There is no fail state, and nothing can drown.
- **Release Day:** when the shark is grown, Arlo opens the gate and it swims to the open ocean. A fact card explains nursery pens, and a new baby shark arrives.

**Keep it gentle.** No animal gets hurt, and anything built can be picked up again.
